# 🍕 Pizza Sales Analysis – Power BI Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI** dashboard that analyses one year (**1 Jan 2015 – 31 Dec 2015**) of pizza sales. It answers which pizzas, categories, and sizes sell best, which days and months are busiest, and which pizzas perform worst.

**Created by:** Uzma Ejaz
**File:** `Pizza_Sales_Analysis_POWER_BI.pbix`

## 🎯 Project Objective
Help a pizza business understand **what sells, when it sells, and what does not**, so it can plan menu, staffing, and promotions.

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- **Power BI Desktop** (data model, DAX measures, interactive visuals)
- **DAX** measures: Total Revenue, Total Orders, Total Pizzas Sold, Avg Order Value, Avg Pizzas Per Order
- Visuals: KPI cards, donut chart, pie chart, funnel chart, column chart, area chart, Top/Bottom 5 bar charts
- Slicers (date range and pizza category) and page navigation buttons

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Home
- **KPI cards:** Total Revenue, Avg Order Value, Total Pizzas Sold, Total Orders, Avg Pizzas Per Order
- **% Sales by Pizza Category** (donut chart)
- **% Sales by Pizza Size** (pie chart)
- **Total Pizzas Sold by Pizza Category** (funnel chart)
- **Total Orders by Order Day** (column chart)
- **Total Orders by Month** (area chart)
- Text panels summarising the busiest days/times and sales performance

### 2. Best / Worst Sellers
- **Top 5 and Bottom 5 pizzas** by Revenue, by Quantity sold, and by Total Orders
- Text panels summarising the best and worst pizza performance

**Interactivity:** Date range slicer and Pizza Category slicer filter every visual on both pages.

## 💡 Key Insights
- **Category:** *Classic* is the top category, with the most pizzas sold (14,888) and 26.91% of sales. Supreme (25.46%), Chicken (23.96%) and Veggie (23.68%) are close behind, so sales are well balanced.
- **Size:** *Large* pizzas bring the most sales (**45.89%**), followed by Medium (30.49%) and Regular (21.77%). X-Large (1.72%) and XX-Large are very small.
- **Busiest days:** **Friday** has the most orders (about 3.5K), followed by Thursday and Saturday (3.2K each). **Sunday** is the slowest (about 2.6K).
- **Busiest months:** **July** (1,935 orders) is the peak, followed by May (1,853) and January (1,845). October and September are the lowest (about 1,650).
- **Best sellers:** *The Thai Chicken Pizza* earns the highest revenue (about 43K). *The Classic* pizza leads in both quantity sold and number of orders.
- **Worst seller:** *The Brie Carre Pizza* is last in revenue (about 12K), quantity (490), and orders (480), far below the other bottom-5 pizzas (about 910–960).

## 🚀 Business Recommendations
- Promote **Large Classic** pizzas, since they are the main driver of sales.
- Run offers on **Sunday and Monday** (lowest order days) to even out demand.
- Staff and stock up for **Thursday–Saturday** evenings and for **July**.
- Review or retire low performers such as *The Brie Carre*, or bundle them with best sellers.
